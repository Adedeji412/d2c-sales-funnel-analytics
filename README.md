# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Funnel & Conversion Analytics

### 📌 Project Overview
![E-Commerce Sales Funnel Dashboard](Screenshot%2026-08-29%230200_1.png)
In the highly competitive direct-to-consumer (D2C) space, traffic is expensive. This project analyzes the complete customer journey of an e-commerce brand—from initial website visit to final purchase—to identify friction points, measure drop-off rates, and uncover revenue leaks. 

By analyzing 120,000 user sessions, this dashboard serves as a diagnostic tool to evaluate the effectiveness of the brand's sales copy, user experience, and overall conversion strategy.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack Used
* **Data Extraction & Aggregation:** MySQL
* **Data Visualization & Modeling:** Microsoft Power BI, DAX
* **Analytical Focus:** Funnel Optimization, Micro-Conversions, Direct Response Metrics

### 📈 Key Insights Discovered
1. **The Cart Abandonment Crisis:** The most glaring bottleneck is a **69.87% cart abandonment rate**. Out of 27,000 users who showed high buying intent by adding an item to their cart, only 8,000 completed the purchase.
2. **Top-of-Funnel Friction:** 35% of all website visitors (42,000 sessions) bounced before viewing a single product, suggesting a disconnect between ad messaging and the landing page experience.
3. **Device Parity:** Cart abandonment is nearly identical across mobile (69.43%) and desktop (70.06%), indicating the checkout friction is a systemic offer/process issue, rather than a mobile-specific technical glitch.

### 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations
To convert these lost sessions into revenue, the brand should implement the following:
* **Deploy Aggressive Retargeting:** Implement an automated direct response email sequence for abandoned carts (e.g., sending a reminder email at 1 hour, and a 10% discount hook at 24 hours). 
* **Optimize Checkout Copy & UX:** A 50% drop-off between "Checkout Started" and "Purchase" requires immediate testing. Recommend adding trust badges, simplifying form fields, and ensuring the sales copy reiterates the core product guarantee right at the point of friction.
* **Revamp the Homepage Hook:** To reduce the initial 35% bounce rate, A/B test the homepage hero section with more magnetic, benefit-driven headlines to immediately capture attention and drive users to product pages.
